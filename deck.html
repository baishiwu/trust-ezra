Revenue Layer 01 — The Hook
Wealth transfer.
Optimizing what
$80T passes on.
The gap
60–70% of homeowning boomer households benefit from a trust over a will. Only 30% have one. A revocable trust costs $3,000–7,000 from an attorney. Upload a tax return. Get a draft trust in minutes.
  • Trust structuring — upload a 1040-SR and Ezra extracts the family's financial picture, identifies probate exposure, and generates a draft trust. Attorney reviews and closes. ~$300 direct fee vs. $3K–7K from an attorney.
  • Account setup — 529s, UTMAs, DAFs, QCDs. Ezra identifies the gap, opens the account, earns the custodian referral. RIA registration converts this to a full advisory relationship at Series A.
  • Beneficiary gaps & document staleness — designations override the will entirely. Ezra cross-references family structure and flags mismatches. Pure subscription value.
  • Later stage — Medicaid asset protection planning, irrevocable trust structures, special needs trusts routed to specialist counsel.

Revenue Layer 02
Medicare.
Pure savings.
No cost. No conflict.
The alignment
Ezra earns a broker commission paid by the carrier at open enrollment — with full incentive to optimize. The family saves. The carrier pays. Ezra earns.
  • Open enrollment execution — Oct 15–Dec 7, annual plan comparison, switch executed in one reply. $100–200 broker commission, carrier-paid, recurring every year.
  • Medicaid & LIS enrollment — many beneficiaries qualify for Low Income Subsidy ("Extra Help") reducing Part D premiums and copays significantly. No commission — pure mission alignment.
  • IRMAA monitoring — income proximity tracking year-round. One dollar over threshold = $2,000+ annual surcharge. Ezra watches so the family doesn't have to.
  • Later stage — Part D formulary optimization as prescriptions evolve; Advantage vs. supplement reassessment when health status shifts.
